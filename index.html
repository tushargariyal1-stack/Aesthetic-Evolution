The V-Taper Goal

Despite starting at 48kg, your goal is to build a V-Taper physique - a powerful aesthetic that creates the illusion of width and strength through strategic muscle development.

Primary Focus Areas

Lateral Delts (Shoulders)

Wide shoulders create the top of the V-shape. Focus on pike pushups and lateral raises.

Pike Pushups Wall Walks
Latissimus Dorsi (Lats)

Wide lats create the downward sweep of the V. Doorway pulls and inverted rows are key.

Doorway Pulls Inverted Rows

V-Taper Building Tips

  • Train shoulders 2x per week for width
  • Focus on back width exercises (pulls/rows)
  • Maintain a narrow waist through core control
  • Stay lean while building muscle for definition
  • Posture improvement enhances the V-effect
